常见问题 FAQ

成片能商用吗？ Can I use the video commercially?

使用范围（平台、地区、期限、是否付费投放）以合同约定为准。音乐用自制的或有商用授权的，交付时附来源清单。天工宇宙的自有角色不随订单转让。
Usage scope (platforms, regions, duration, paid ads) follows the contract. Music is made in-house or commercially licensed, with a source list at delivery. TiangongVerse's own characters are not transferred with orders.

会标注 AI 吗？ Is AI content labeled?

会。AI 生成的画面都挂「AI 生成 / AI-generated」标识，平台要求的 AI 标识也不会去掉。
Yes. AI-generated visuals carry an “AI 生成 / AI-generated” label, and we never remove platform-required AI labels.

修改怎么算？ How do revisions work?

A、B 档含 1 次修改，C、D 档含 2 次，分镜和成片阶段合计。一次修改指：您把意见一次性汇总发来，我们改一轮、交回一版。范围限于已确认的脚本和方向；推翻已确认的方向按新需求另算。我们自己的错误改正不计次数。超出的修改每次收该档单价的 25%，最低 ¥50 / $10。
Packages A and B include 1 revision round; C and D include 2, counted across storyboard and final cut. One round = you send all feedback in one consolidated message, and we deliver one revised version. Rounds cover changes within the approved script and direction; changing an approved direction counts as a new brief. Fixes for our own mistakes don't count. Extra rounds cost 25% of the package price each (min ¥50 / $10).

能加急吗？ Can you rush it?

可以 48 小时内交付，加收整单价的 50%，从素材齐全、脚本确认那一刻算起。当天交付不接。
Yes: delivery within 48 hours for +50% of the package price, counted from when materials are in and the script is approved. Same-day delivery is not available.

能退款吗？ Refunds?

按合同约定。
As stated in the contract.

AI 能做到多精准？ How accurate is AI?

AI 画面在人物和产品细节上可能有轻微不一致，做不到逐帧完美。产品片以实拍图为准，不改货。
AI visuals may show slight inconsistencies; frame-perfect consistency isn't possible. Product films are built from your real photos without altering the product.

哪些内容不接？ What don't you take on?

真人换脸、冒用他人肖像、侵权内容、医疗或功效宣传、政治内容。
Real-person face swaps, unauthorized likenesses, infringing content, medical or health-benefit claims, and political content.
